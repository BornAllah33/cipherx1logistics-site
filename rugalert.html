What It Catches

A rug pull isn't magic — it's a loaded gun anyone can read on-chain: liquidity that isn't locked, a handful of wallets holding the float, a contract owner who kept the mint keys, and often a throwaway deployer with a trail of dead tokens behind them.

RugAlert reads all of it, scores it across three independent lenses, and tells you exactly which conditions fired. It doesn't accuse a token or promise it's safe — it shows you the structural risk and lets you decide. For trading bots, it's the pre-trade gate: ask before you ape.

It never signs, holds a key, or moves a cent. It only ever reads public data and reports what it sees.

How It Scores — Three Lenses

🧱Pool structure

Liquidity lock, top-holder concentration, on-chain tx velocity, and whether the owner still holds privilege keys — the four classic rug vectors.

🕵️Deployer reputation

How old the launching account is and whether it's launched-and-dumped before. A clean pool from a serial rugger isn't clean.

⭐Bot score

Pulls the on-chain agent reputation (ProtonNZ's agentfeed) so you score the counterparty, not just the pool.
