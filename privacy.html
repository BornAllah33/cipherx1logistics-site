Privacy Policy

CipherX1 Logistics · effective November 9, 2026

CipherX1 Logistics builds security tools — RugAlert, PayShield, Bot Blockk, KeyGuardian, Rust Matrix, and CosmicRust. This policy covers all of them. The short version: our apps do not collect your personal data. We built them read-only and non-custodial on purpose, so there is very little about you for us to have.

What we collect

Nothing, by default. Our apps do not create accounts, do not require sign-in to use their core function, and do not run analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Data the apps read (not collect)

  • Public blockchain data. RugAlert and our other on-chain tools read publicly available data from the XPR Network (for example a token's market, the account that launched it, public reputation scores). This is public information on a public ledger. We read it; we do not store it about you.
  • Read-only and non-custodial. Our apps never hold your private keys, never sign transactions for you, and never move funds. Any payment you make is a transaction you sign in your own wallet.

If you buy a paid tier

Upgrades are optional and happen on our website, not inside the mobile apps. When you pay, you sign a transaction in your own WebAuth wallet to our account yacubai on the XPR Network. Because it is a blockchain payment:

  • The transaction — your wallet account, the amount, and the memo — is recorded on the public XPR ledger by the network, not by us. Anyone can see on-chain transactions; that is how the chain works.
  • You include an email address in the memo so we can activate your license. We use that email only to deliver and support your license. We do not sell it, rent it, or add it to marketing lists.
  • Your license is verified either on-chain or with an offline signed key on your own device. We do not run a server that tracks your usage.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • We never access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.
  • We never ask for your seed phrase or private keys. No legitimate app ever will.

Third parties

To show live prices and read public chain data, the apps may call public API endpoints (for example a price feed and public XPR Network RPC nodes). These requests carry no personal information beyond what any app needs to make a network request. We do not control those providers' own policies.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
