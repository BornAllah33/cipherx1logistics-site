What We Do

AI agents are starting to run real operations — moving money, touching files, executing commands, signing transactions. The moment you hand an agent that power, a single bad instruction can drain a wallet, leak a key, or wipe a drive. The tools to watch and contain that power didn't exist yet.

CipherX1 Logistics builds them. We make defense-grade security and utility software that sits between autonomous AI and the real world — on your machine, under your control. No cloud, no middleman, no trust required. We gate the action, not just the text: what an agent is about to do is checked before it can happen.

Our focus is logistics, healthcare, and agentic finance — traditional finance, DeFi, and post-quantum-secured — the places where an automated mistake costs real money or exposes private data — but everything we ship works for anyone who runs AI near something that matters.

Our Lane

Others secure the rails. We secure the people and the AI agents riding them.

Blockchains, exchanges and infrastructure security protect the networks — the chains, the bridges, the contracts. That work matters. That's our lane. CipherX1 protects the AI agents themselves and the people who own them — on their own hardware, at the exact moment an action is about to happen. Every product we ship is built for agentic AI use: a person, a team, or an autonomous agent acting on someone's behalf.

The rails keep the network honest. We keep the agent — and its owner — safe on it. Built for the agentic era from day one.

Questions

How do I get updates?

Linux (Pop!_OS / Ubuntu): installing any of our .deb files also turns on our signed update source, so new versions arrive with your normal system updates — the Pop!_Shop, or sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade. Windows & Mac: the app shows a banner when a new version is out; click it, download the new installer from this site, and install it over the old one. Your activation and settings stay on your computer, so you don't activate again. Updates only ever come from cipherx1logistics.com — never from a link someone sends you.

Is it safe to install?

Yes. Every app is built by CipherX1 and downloads only from cipherx1logistics.com. Our Windows and Mac installers aren't code-signed yet (that's in progress), so Windows may say "unknown publisher" — click More info → Run anyway. On a Mac, if it's blocked the first time, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Why don't you ask for my email?

Because we don't need it — and data we don't collect can never leak. No email, no account, no tracking. Your apps run on your machine, and crypto purchases are proven on the public chain — not stored in our database.

How does the free trial work?

Install Rust Matrix, Bot Blockk, PayShield or Agentic Rust Coding Loop and the 8-day trial starts on its own — core features, one trial per computer. After that it drops to a basic mode until you activate. KeyGuardian and RugAlert have free-forever tiers instead.

What's an activation code?

When you pay with crypto, each app shows a short code made just for your computer. Paste it at checkout and it goes with your payment, so your purchase unlocks your copy. Then press Activate in the app — it checks the public XPR chain and unlocks. Nothing else is sent.

How do I know I'm on the real site?

Only buy at cipherx1logistics.com. We will never DM you for payment, ask for your seed phrase or private key, or send you to another site to pay. Anyone who does is a scammer — report them.
