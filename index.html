What We Do

AI agents are starting to run real operations — moving money, touching files, executing commands, signing transactions. The moment you hand an agent that power, a single bad instruction can drain a wallet, leak a key, or wipe a drive. The tools to watch and contain that power didn't exist yet.

CipherX1 Logistics builds them. We make defense-grade security and utility software that sits between autonomous AI and the real world — on your machine, under your control. No cloud, no middleman, no trust required. We gate the action, not just the text: what an agent is about to do is checked before it can happen.

Our focus is logistics, finance, and healthcare — the places where an automated mistake costs real money or exposes private data — but everything we ship works for anyone who runs AI near something that matters.

How We Build

→Local-first

Everything runs on your own device. No cloud, no account, no telemetry — your data and documents never leave your machine.

→Deny-by-default

Nothing executes unless it's explicitly safe. The dangerous move is stopped before it happens, not flagged after.

→Non-custodial

We never hold your keys, your funds, or your files. You sign your own transactions in your own wallet. Always.

→Defense-grade

Rust-native, memory-safe, tamper-evident, with optional hardware (YubiKey) root of trust. Built to be hardened, not patched.
